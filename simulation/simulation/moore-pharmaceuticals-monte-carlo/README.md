## Executive Summary

Pharmaceutical investments face uncertainty in adoption, costs, and regulatory success.

Monte Carlo simulation evaluates distribution outcomes rather than single-point NPV estimates.

---

## Decision Problem

Should the company invest in drug development given uncertain adoption and cost outcomes?

---

## Approach

Demand, adoption, and cost variables are simulated across thousands of scenarios.

NPV distributions are generated to quantify risk exposure.

---

## Impact / Key Findings

Results show significant downside risk hidden by single NPV calculations.

Decision-making improves when risk distribution is visible.

---

## 📐 Mathematical Formulation

\[
NPV = \sum \frac{CashFlow_t}{(1+r)^t} - Investment
\]

Variables are simulated stochastically.
